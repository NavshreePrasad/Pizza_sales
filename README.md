# Pizza_sales
Data Analytics Project showcasing pizza sales analysis using Python, SQL and Power BI

# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end **Pizza Sales Analysis** project that leverages **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze transactional sales data, identify revenue drivers, evaluate customer purchasing patterns, and uncover actionable business insights. The project transforms raw sales data into interactive dashboards that support data-driven business decisions for inventory planning, sales optimization, and operational efficiency.

---

# 📖 Short Description

The **Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution designed to help pizza businesses monitor sales performance, analyze customer purchasing behavior, identify best-selling products, and track sales trends across categories, sizes, and time periods.

Using transactional sales data, the project provides valuable insights into revenue generation, order patterns, product performance, and customer preferences. It combines **Python for data preparation, SQL for business analysis, and Power BI for interactive visualization**, delivering a complete end-to-end analytics workflow.

---

# 🛠 Tech Stack

The project was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 🐍 **Python** – Data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering, and exploratory data analysis
- 📊 **Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation and transformation
- 📈 **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Exploratory data visualization
- 🗄️ **SQL (MySQL)** – Business queries and analytical reporting
- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Interactive dashboard development
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Data transformation and loading
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and dynamic measures
- 📝 **Jupyter Notebook** – Python development environment
- 📄 **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** – Project planning and business objectives
- 📁 **CSV Dataset** – Source transactional sales data

# 📂 Data Source

**Dataset:** Pizza Sales Dataset

The dataset contains transactional pizza sales records, including customer orders, pizza details, pricing, quantities, and timestamps. It enables comprehensive sales, product, and time-series analysis.

### Dataset Includes

- Order ID
- Pizza ID
- Pizza Name
- Pizza Category
- Pizza Size
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Price
- Order Date
- Order Time

# ✨ Features & Highlights

## 📌 Business Problem

Pizza businesses generate thousands of sales transactions, making it difficult to manually monitor performance, identify customer preferences, and optimize inventory and staffing. Management requires a centralized reporting solution to evaluate sales performance, identify high-performing products, analyze customer purchasing trends, and make informed operational decisions.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard was developed to:

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track revenue and order trends
- Identify best-selling and least-selling pizzas
- Understand customer purchasing patterns
- Evaluate pizza category and size performance
- Monitor business KPIs
- Support inventory, staffing, and marketing decisions

---

# 📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

### 🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard displays important business KPIs including:

- 💰 **Total Revenue**
- 🍕 **Total Pizzas Sold**
- 🛒 **Total Orders**
- 💵 **Average Order Value (AOV)**
- 📦 **Average Pizzas per Order**

---

### 🔹 Daily Sales Trend

Visualizes total orders by day of the week to identify customer purchasing patterns and peak business days.

**Business Value**

- Supports staffing decisions
- Identifies high-demand days
- Improves operational planning

---

### 🔹 Monthly Sales Trend

Tracks monthly order volume to identify seasonal sales patterns and business performance over time.

**Business Value**

- Identifies peak and low sales months
- Supports promotional planning
- Enables demand forecasting

---

### 🔹 Hourly Sales Trend

Analyzes order distribution throughout the day to understand customer traffic patterns.

**Business Value**

- Optimizes staffing schedules
- Improves kitchen operations
- Supports inventory preparation during peak hours

---

### 🔹 Sales by Pizza Category

Displays the percentage contribution of each pizza category toward total revenue.

Provides insights into customer preferences across:

- 🍕 Classic
- 🥬 Veggie
- 🍗 Chicken
- 👑 Supreme

---

### 🔹 Sales by Pizza Size

Compares revenue generated across different pizza sizes.

**Key Insight**

Large pizzas contribute the highest percentage of total revenue.

---

### 🔹 Top 5 Best-Selling Pizzas

Highlights the highest-performing pizzas based on:

- Revenue
- Quantity Sold
- Total Orders

Helping management identify products that drive business growth.

---

### 🔹 Bottom 5 Least-Selling Pizzas

Identifies pizzas with the lowest sales performance, enabling management to review pricing, promotions, or menu optimization.

---

### 🔹 Ingredient Analysis

Analyzes ingredient usage across pizza varieties to understand inventory consumption and support purchasing decisions.

---

### 🔹 Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter dashboard visuals by:

- Pizza Category
- Pizza Size
- Order Date
- Month
- Day of Week

allowing users to explore sales performance interactively.
